Updated Prompt — Mashujaa Voices UI

Prompt:
Design a clean, modern, and emotionally resonant web interface for an AI cultural storytelling platform called “Mashujaa Voices.”
The platform allows users to upload historical photos and get an AI-generated story and audio narration about the image — celebrating Kenya’s heroes and heritage.

🌍 Overall Theme

The interface should feel modern yet patriotic, blending heritage with technology.

Color Palette (Kenyan-inspired, but modernized):

Deep black (#0D0D0D) — strength and history

Rich red (#B40000) — sacrifice and valor

Deep green (#1C4B2B) — growth and hope

Soft cream or ivory (#F8F5E9) — warmth and storytelling backdrop

Hints of metallic gold (#D4AF37) — to signify honor and legacy

Typography: Elegant sans-serif font (e.g. Inter, Poppins, or Source Sans).

Imagery: Blend AI-generated visuals with real historical photos in subtle background layers or hero sections.

🏠 Home Page

Hero Section:

Tagline: “Where Kenya’s Past Speaks Again.”

Subtext: “Upload a historical image. Let AI narrate its story.”

Prominent Upload Image button.

Soft ambient background animation or gradient with gentle flag-wave motion (black → red → green).

Optionally, a soft background sound toggle (e.g., gentle piano or ambient drums).

Carousel Section:

Below the hero, display a carousel of existing images already uploaded to the system.

Each card shows:

The image thumbnail

Short title (e.g. “Wangari Maathai, 1984”)

When clicked → user navigates to the Result Page where they can read and hear the audio narration.

Smooth horizontal scroll with autoplay, fade transitions, and overlay captions.

Modern carousel indicators (small circles or progress bars).

Info Section (below carousel):

Brief description of the project’s mission:
“Mashujaa Voices preserves Kenya’s untold stories using AI.
Every photo carries a voice. Every voice carries a legacy.”

📤 Upload Page

Central upload card with a drag-and-drop zone and a Select Image button.

Optional input: “Describe what you know about this image (optional).”

Primary button: Generate Story with a circular loading animation.

While generating → animated text like “Analyzing your image… discovering Kenya’s story…”

📖 Result Page

Split layout with immersive storytelling style:

Left panel: Uploaded image (with light frame or paper texture).

Right panel:

Title (e.g. “Dedan Kimathi, 1956”)

Full AI-generated historical story in a scrollable box.

Audio player with waveform animation and play/pause controls.

Buttons: Regenerate Story, Download Audio, Share Story.

Subtle highlight effect when narration plays (e.g., slight glowing border).

🖼️ Gallery Page

Grid of cards showing community-uploaded images and titles.

Hover → short 2–3 second narration preview.

Filters by hero name, event, region, or decade.

Smooth lazy loading and responsive grid.

🧭 About Page

Full-width section with text overlay:
“Mashujaa Voices is an AI-powered archive preserving Kenya’s stories — one image at a time.”

Subtle cultural symbols (e.g., shield, drums, books) integrated in minimalist form.

Include a “Contribute” button inviting people to share more photos.

🪄 Design Style

Clean, minimal, with soft transitions (Framer Motion style).

Use drop shadows and depth subtly to evoke storytelling layers.

Rounded corners, modern icons, and lots of white/cream space.

Responsive and mobile-friendly layout.

Goal:
To create a living digital museum that feels both technologically advanced and deeply Kenyan.
A place where users can not only upload images but listen to history retold by AI, wrapped in color, sound, and national pride.
